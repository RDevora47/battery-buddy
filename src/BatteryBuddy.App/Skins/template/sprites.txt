# Battery Buddy — the fluffy template pet. Not a shipped pet: copy this folder to start a new one.
# Every sprite is @2x: each character is half an art pixel. skin.json positions are in art pixels.
#
# A guideline, not a rule: start from it, then let each pet be itself (longer ears, a muzzle, a crest...).
#
# What makes a pet fluffy and adorable:
#   - A round, ball-shaped head: nearly a circle (22 art pixels wide, 17 tall, from row 3 to row 19),
#     big enough that the body only peeks out below it. No flat sides, no corners, no tufts.
#   - Fur (p) on the top half of the ball, light fluff (t) on the cheeks and chin, and a soft shade (h)
#     along the lower rim so it reads as round.
#   - Big eyes (3x3 art pixels), a little below the middle of the ball and set wide apart, each with two
#     white glints and a coloured iris along the bottom. Blinking eyes close into soft ‿ arcs.
#   - A small nose between the eyes and an ω mouth right under it. Blush (v) under each eye.
#   - Ears are triangles standing on the curve of the crown. Their base blends into the fur (no outline
#     across it); they tilt outward as the battery drains and flop down over the sides of the ball when low.
#   - Paws in the fluff colour with little toe lines, resting on the bottom of the ball.
#
# Making a pet from it: copy skin.json and sprites.txt, change the palette below, paint markings (a mask,
# patches, stripes) over the fur and reshape whatever the animal needs. The skin tests say when a change
# moves the hats, earbuds or tail out of place.
